# Festival Assignment — Stakeholder Questions

**Discovery guide: Drafted. Organization Review: Pending.**

Companion to the [Organization-Facing Proposal](organization-facing-proposal.md)
and [Meeting Narrative](meeting-narrative.md). Select relevant prompts to turn
modeled assumptions into validated operating information. Questions explore
policy and operating needs; they do not prescribe solutions or request approval
of the current fairness formula.

Use aggregate counts, distributions and non-personal layouts where possible.
Current modeled population and capacity numbers are not confirmed organization
values. Record organization-specific answers and any confidential supporting
material privately, outside this repository.

## Attendance and Capacity

- How many Attendees are typically registered for each FestivalDay, and how many
  usually seek a location that day?
- What is the usable assignment capacity, and does it vary by day?
- Are all physical locations assignable? Which locations are excluded from that
  usable capacity?

## Current Allocation / Queue Process

- How do Attendees currently obtain locations?
- What role does physical arrival order play, and how early do people typically queue?
- What works well, and what operational difficulties, if any, does the organization observe?
- Which exceptions do staff currently handle manually?

## Request Timing

- When could Attendees request a location, and when would requests close?
- Would everyone be allowed to request at the same time, or could opening be
  organized into time windows?
- Could requests for different FestivalDays be made in advance?
- Are there known peak periods or short bursts of demand?

These answers inform whether further arrival-concurrency validation is relevant.

## Groups

- What are typical group sizes, and what is the largest expected group?
- Must every group remain together, and what does togetherness mean operationally?
- If a group cannot be assigned, may it split or change membership, and may its
  members resubmit requests?
- May organization staff restructure groups? Under what operating rules?

These answers determine the relevance and scope of deferred late-large-group
and reconfiguration validation; splitting and resubmission are not assumed.

## Venue and Inventory

- How are Zones, Rows and Spot numbering represented in the venue layout? A Spot
  means an individual assignable location, including where seating is not used.
- What counts as physically contiguous, including across aisles, row boundaries
  or standing areas?
- Which locations may be blocked or temporarily unavailable?
- Can inventory change during a FestivalDay, and how are manual changes currently handled?

## Reserved / Special Areas

- Which areas, if any, are reserved for staff, volunteers, accessible locations,
  VIP or other special allocations?
- How are organization-controlled reservations and other blocked areas distributed
  across the venue?
- Can reservations or special-area availability change during the day?

These answers inform usable capacity and fragmentation modeling; a total free
Spot count alone does not establish contiguous group availability.

## Fairness Expectations

- What does a fair allocation mean to the organization?
- Should less favorable previous experiences increase priority on later days?
- How should equal opportunity across days and strict arrival order be balanced?
- How should repeated favorable experiences be treated?
- Should any groups receive special eligibility, and what business rules would govern it?

## Operational Exceptions

- How are late arrivals and no-shows handled today?
- What needs arise around cancelled requests or manual reassignment?
- What happens when sections close or an emergency affects available locations?
- When are staff overrides needed, and what decisions do they affect?

These questions discover needs without designing exceptions. The current model
keeps completed Assignments final for that FestivalDay; any different policy
would need separate review.

## Performance Expectations

- What response time would be considered acceptable during ordinary and peak periods?
- What should happen operationally if assignment processing is temporarily unavailable?
- Is synchronous confirmation required, or would a short queued/waiting state be acceptable?

These expectations will inform later concurrency architecture evaluation; no
coordination strategy or service-level guarantee is selected here.

## Pilot / Adoption

- Would the organization consider a limited pilot after the required validation
  and formal review?
- Which day, section or population would be safest to consider for a pilot?
- What evidence would be needed before broader adoption?
- Which roles would need to approve operating-rule changes?

This public proposal package is maintained in English. Organization-specific
meeting material may later be translated and adapted privately for the relevant
stakeholders.
Future Spanish discovery notes or meeting documents belong outside the public
repository; this guide contains no actual organization answers or approvals.
